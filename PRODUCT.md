# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single learner reviewing machine-learning concepts, typically studying on a laptop at home or at work and occasionally reviewing on a phone. The user needs to move from broad revision into active recall without configuring an account or learning a complex tool. Visible wording should describe neutral professional learning, not a job search.

## Product Purpose

Turn a large ML Q&A guide into a local, interactive study environment. Success means the learner can quickly decide what to study next, practice one question at a time, track honest mastery, revisit weak material, and maintain momentum across sessions without cloud services.

## Brand Personality

Focused, encouraging, and intellectually playful. The interface should feel like a well-run training room: serious about progress, calm under pressure, and satisfying to use.

## Anti-references

Avoid childish classroom themes, casino-like rewards, manipulative streak pressure, noisy achievement popups, generic SaaS dashboards, dense documentation pages, and decorative AI imagery. Gamification must clarify learning rather than distract from it.

## Design Principles

1. Active recall over passive reading: reveal answers only after the learner attempts a response.
2. Honest progress over vanity metrics: mastery comes from self-rating and repeated success, not page views.
3. One clear next action: every screen should make the next useful study step obvious.
4. Depth without overwhelm: preserve all source material while revealing it in manageable sessions.
5. Local and durable: progress works offline, survives refreshes, and can be reset or exported by the learner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, screen-reader status updates, non-color status cues, comfortable reading widths, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. Do not punish missed days or rely on red-green distinctions alone.
